# APRSWolf v1.1.0

Release date:

- 2026-10-12

Primary download:

- `APRSWolf_1.1.0_x64.dmg` (Intel Mac) or `APRSWolf_1.1.0_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon Mac)

## What's New

- Sent and received messages are now kept in a local SQLite database, so your history survives restarts and can be searched.
- One search field filters both the Sent and Received panes across the whole history.
- Messages can be hidden or deleted, one at a time, as a selection, or everything a list shows.
- A red unread count appears on the `To Me` filter and on the app icon for messages addressed to you that arrive while APRSWolf is in the background.
- The main window is reworked: the send bar is on top, a compact status bar sits at the bottom, and the header shows UTC and local time.
- The pop-out is now a Messages window that shows sent and received messages in one timeline.

## User-Facing Changes

- New:
  Message history is stored in `messages.db` in the app data folder, with a stable UUID per message. Sent and received messages, queries, and bulletins are kept until you delete them; positions, weather, telemetry, status, objects, items, Mic-E, and acks are trimmed after 7 days by default. Preferences > Message History offers 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or keep everything.
- New:
  A single search field in the Sent Messages header runs full-text search over sent and received history and filters both panes as you type. The Messages window has its own search field.
- New:
  Messages can be cleared with an `×` on hover, a long-press selection, or `Clear all` (which clears what the pane shows, up to the newest 200). The dialog's `Delete from database` box is unchecked by default: unchecked hides the message but search still finds it, marked `HIDDEN`, with `↺` to unhide it; checked deletes it.
- New:
  Messages addressed to you that arrive while APRSWolf is in the background show a red count on the `To Me` filter and on the app icon. Both clear when an APRSWolf window has focus, and Preferences can turn the app icon count off.
- New:
  The header shows UTC and local time as 24-hour `hh:mm` with the local zone's short name (for example `15:08Z 08:08 PDT`).
- New:
  The pop-out is now a Messages window that lists sent and received messages in one newest-first timeline, with sent messages in purple and their delivery status shown.
- Improved:
  The main window is reworked: the send bar moves to the top with a normal-size Send button, the Sent and Received panes sit below it, and Station, TNC, APRS-IS, and Remote Web Control share one compact bar at the bottom, each with a green or red status dot and its current setting. The mutual-exclusion note moved into tooltips on the titles.
- Improved:
  Preferences opens from a gear at the top right, closes with Escape, and sizes to fit every setting.
- Improved:
  Sent messages keep their delivery status across restarts. A send still waiting for its ack when the app closes is marked expired at the next launch, since the retry queue starts empty.
- Improved:
  A packet heard twice within 30 seconds (over RF and APRS-IS, or through two digipeaters) is stored once, fingerprinted on sender, addressee, body, and message number. The Traffic Log still shows every copy, tagged `(duplicate)`.
- Improved:
  The old `received_messages.json` is imported once on first launch and renamed `received_messages.json.migrated`.

## Upgrade Notes

- On first launch after upgrading, the old `received_messages.json` is imported once into the new database and renamed `received_messages.json.migrated`. Your existing received history is preserved.
- The message database schema migrates automatically by `user_version` (now 3). No manual action is required.
- Message retention defaults to 7 days for positions, weather, telemetry, status, objects, items, Mic-E, and acks. Change it under Preferences > Message History if you want to keep them longer or shorter. Messages, queries, and bulletins are kept until you delete them.
- The app icon unread count is on by default. Turn it off in Preferences if you do not want it.
- On Linux, the app icon count uses the Tauri badge API, which KDE Plasma and the Ubuntu dock display and stock GNOME does not. On Windows, the taskbar icon shows a red dot overlay instead of a number.

Note: the macOS `aarch64` DMG is for Apple Silicon Macs. The Linux `arm64`
packages are for ARM64 Linux (for example a 64-bit Raspberry Pi). They are not
interchangeable.

## Known Issues

- macOS and Windows installers are not Developer ID signed or notarized and may require the first-launch confirmation described in the end-user guide.
- The unread app icon count has been tested on macOS only. Windows and Linux are covered by code review: Linux depends on the desktop environment (KDE Plasma and the Ubuntu dock show it; stock GNOME does not), and Windows shows a red dot overlay instead of a number.
